⚠️ Limitations of BMI

  • Does not distinguish between muscle and fat
  • Athletes may have high BMI due to muscle mass
  • Does not account for fat distribution
  • May not be accurate for all ethnicities
  • Not suitable for children under 2
  • Should not be the only health measure used
