🔄 APA 7th Key Changes

  • Up to 20 authors listed (was 7)
  • DOI format: https://doi.org/xxxxx
  • No publisher city needed
  • Include issue numbers for all journals
  • "Retrieved from" no longer used for most URLs

📝 Common Citation Patterns

  • One author: (Smith, 2023)
  • Two authors: (Smith & Jones, 2023)
  • 3+ authors: (Smith et al., 2023)
  • Direct quote: (Smith, 2023, p. 45)
  • Group author: (World Health Organization, 2023)
