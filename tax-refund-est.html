📘How Tax Refunds Work

At the end of the tax year (31 March), IRD compares the PAYE your employer deducted with your actual tax liability. If too much was deducted, IRD refunds the difference. If too little, you receive a tax bill.

💡KiwiSaver & Student Loan

KiwiSaver contributions are deducted from your after-tax pay and do not affect your tax refund. Student Loan repayments (12% of income over the threshold) are included in the calculation as they are deducted through PAYE.
