📘How Child Support Works

Child support in NZ is calculated using a formula based on both parents' taxable incomes, the number of children, and the care arrangement. The goal is to assess each parent's ability to pay.

📐IRD Formula

Step 1: Calculate Child Support Income

CSI = Taxable Income - Living Allowance

Step 2: Apply income percentage

1 child: 18%, 2: 24%, 3: 27%, 4+: 30%

Step 3: Adjust for care

28-51% care: x0.50, 52-72%: x0.35, 73%+: x0

* This is a simplified estimate. Actual IRD assessment may vary.

📋Living Allowances (2026/2027)

  • Single (no partner): $24,128
  • Living with partner: $18,144

These are the thresholds for child support income calculation.

🔗Official Reference

All child support rules are set by Inland Revenue (IRD).
