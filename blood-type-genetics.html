🧬 ABO System Explained

The ABO blood group system is determined by the presence or absence of A and B antigens on red blood cells:

AAAnti-B

🔬 Rh Factor

  • Rh positive (+) means the RhD antigen is present
  • Rh negative (−) means the RhD antigen is absent
  • Rh+ is dominant; Rh− is recessive
  • An Rh+ parent can carry a hidden Rh− allele
  • Important in pregnancy: Rh− mother + Rh+ baby may require treatment
