📘How It Works

Compares long-term financial impact of buying vs renting in New Zealand. Models net wealth (assets minus costs) over time.

🏠Buying Costs Modelled

  • Mortgage interest (monthly amortization)
  • Council rates (~0.35% p.a.)
  • House insurance (~0.25% p.a.)
  • Maintenance (1% of value p.a.)
  • Opportunity cost on deposit

🔑Renting Costs Modelled

  • Annual rent (with yearly increases)
  • Contents insurance (~$400/yr)
  • Offset by investment returns on deposit
