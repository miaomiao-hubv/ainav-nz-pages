📝 NZ Legal Requirements for E-Signatures

Under the Electronic Transactions Act 2002, electronic signatures are legally recognised in New Zealand when the signatory intends to sign and the method is reliable. This tool meets those requirements by creating a traceable, embedded signature.

⚖️ Electronic Transactions Act 2002

  • E-signatures are valid for most NZ business transactions
  • The signatory must intend the signature to be binding
  • The method must be reliable and appropriately identify the person
  • Some documents (wills, affidavits) still require wet-ink signatures
