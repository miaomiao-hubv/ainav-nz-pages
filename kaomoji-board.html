🇯🇵 What are Kaomoji?

Kaomoji (顔文字, "face characters") are Japanese-style emoticons built from text characters. Unlike Western sideways emoticons like :) , kaomoji are viewed upright and can express complex emotions with creative combinations of symbols.

📝 How to Use

Click any kaomoji card to copy it to your clipboard. Then paste it anywhere — messages, social media, emails. Kaomoji work on all modern platforms and devices. Try mixing them for unique expressions!
