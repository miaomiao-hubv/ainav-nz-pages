📖 How to Read the Table

  • Rows are called periods (1-7)
  • Columns are called groups (1-18)
  • Elements in the same group share similar properties
  • Atomic number increases left to right across a period
  • Lanthanides and actinides are placed below for space

🎨 Element Categories Legend

  • Alkali metals: Highly reactive, Group 1
  • Noble gases: Inert, Group 18
  • Halogens: Reactive nonmetals, Group 17
  • Transition metals: Groups 3-12
  • Lanthanides/Actinides: Bottom two rows
