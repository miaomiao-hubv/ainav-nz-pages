📘About Working for Families

Working for Families provides tax credits to support NZ families with dependent children. Includes Family Tax Credit, In-work Tax Credit, Minimum Family Tax Credit, and Best Start.

📐2026/2027 Rates

  • Family Tax Credit: $144/week (eldest), $111/week (others)
  • In-work Tax Credit: $72.50/week (eligible families)
  • Best Start: $73.75/week (first year), then abates
  • Abatement threshold: $42,700 (27% abatement rate)
  • Best Start abatement: $79,000 (21% for year 2-3)

📋Eligibility

  • Family Tax Credit: any family with dependent children
  • In-work Tax Credit: 20+ hrs (single) or 30+ hrs (couple)
  • Best Start: for each child under 3 years old
  • Abatement applies above income threshold

🔗Official Reference

All rates and rules are set by Inland Revenue (IRD).
