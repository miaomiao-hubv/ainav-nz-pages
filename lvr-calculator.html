📘What is LVR?

LVR = (Loan ÷ Property Value) × 100. Measures how much of the property is mortgaged. Lower LVR = lower risk for lenders.

📐LVR Formula

Calculate LVR:

LVR = (Loan / Value) × 100

Deposit:

Deposit = Value − Loan

🏦RBNZ LVR Restrictions (2026)

  • Owner-occupier: max 20% of new loans to high-LVR (>80%)
  • Investor: max 5% to high-LVR (>70%)
  • LVR ≤ 80% (owner) or ≤ 70% (investor) = standard pricing

💡Low Equity Margin

If LVR > 80% (owner) or > 70% (investor), banks typically charge a Low Equity Margin (LEM) of 0.25%–1.00% above standard rate.
