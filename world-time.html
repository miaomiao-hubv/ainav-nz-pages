🇳🇿 NZ Time Zones

NZST (New Zealand Standard Time): UTC+12 — observed April–September.
NZDT (New Zealand Daylight Time): UTC+13 — observed September–April.

⏰ Daylight Saving Info

NZ clocks go forward 1 hour on the last Sunday of September and back on the first Sunday of April. Not all countries observe DST.

🌐 International Date Line

NZ is one of the first countries to see each new day. The IDL passes through the Pacific, placing NZ UTC+12/+13 — ahead of most of the world.
