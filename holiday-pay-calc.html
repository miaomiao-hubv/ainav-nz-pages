💡Pay-as-You-Go vs Accrual

  • 8% PAYG: Paid with each pay (casuals)
  • Accrual: Leave accrues gradually (permanent)
  • Unpaid leave paid out at job end
  • Public holidays separate from annual leave

📘About Holiday Pay

Most NZ employees get 4 weeks paid annual leave. Casual workers typically receive 8% of gross earnings with each pay or at end of employment.
