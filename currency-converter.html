🇳🇿NZ Dollar Info

The New Zealand Dollar (NZD) is the 10th most traded currency worldwide. Key facts:

  • NZD/USD is the most traded pair
  • Trading hours: NZ market 9am–5pm NZST (UTC+12)
  • Overlaps with Sydney & Tokyo sessions
  • Often called the "Kiwi" after the NZ bird on the $1 coin
  • Managed by the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ)
