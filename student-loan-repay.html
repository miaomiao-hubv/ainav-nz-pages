📘How Student Loan Repayment Works

Student loan repayments are deducted automatically from your income if you earn above the repayment threshold. The rate is 12% on every dollar earned above the annual threshold of $24,128 (2026/2027 tax year).

📐Key Formula

Repayment calculation:

Repayment = (Income − Threshold) × 12%

Example ($50,000 income):

($50,000 − $24,128) × 0.12 = $3,104.64

No repayment if income is below the threshold.

📋Key Information

  • Threshold: $24,128 (2026/2027)
  • Repayment rate: 12% above threshold
  • Deducted automatically via PAYE
  • Self-employed: pay via income tax return
  • No interest on loans for NZ-based borrowers

🔗Official Reference

All student loan repayment rules are set by Inland Revenue (IRD).
