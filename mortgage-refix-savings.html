📌 What is "Refix"?

When your fixed-rate period ends, you "refix" — choosing a new fixed term and rate. Banks often offer different rates for 1, 2, or 3-year terms.

💡 Why Refix Now?

The OCR has been cut multiple times in 2024-2025. Many banks are now offering lower fixed rates. Refixing at a lower rate can save you thousands over the loan term.

⚠️ Break Fees

If you break your current fixed term early, your bank may charge a "break fee". Check with your bank before making changes.
