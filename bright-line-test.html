📘What Is Bright-Line?

Taxes gains from selling residential property if sold within a specified "bright-line period" after purchase.

📐Bright-Line Periods

<27 Mar 2021: 5-year rule
27 Mar – 30 Jun 2024: 10 years (5 for new builds)
From 1 Jul 2024: 2 years (5 for new builds before Jul 2024)

🏠Main Home Exemption

  • Main home = full exemption even if within bright-line
  • Can only use once every 2 years per person
  • Land >4,500m² may not qualify

🆕New Build Exemption

  • New builds: 5-year window (during 10-year era)
  • "New build" = consent issued <1 year before title
  • From 1 Jul 2024: standard 2-year applies to all
ird.govt.nz/bright-line

⚠️Important Notes

  • Guide only — consult a tax advisor
  • Period measured from title registration to S&PA date
  • Inherited / relationship property may be exempt
  • Offshore persons may have different rules
