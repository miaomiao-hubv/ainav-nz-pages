🎰KiwiSaver Details

New Zealand's retirement savings scheme:

  • Employee contribution: 3% default (can choose 4%, 6%, 8%, 10%)
  • Employer contribution: 3% (minus PIE tax)
  • Government annual credit: up to NZ$521 (if earning NZ$34,000+)
  • Prescribed Investor Rate (PIR): 28% default
  • First-home withdrawal available after 3 years

Fund types:

  • Conservative — low risk, lower returns
  • Balanced — medium risk, moderate returns
  • Growth — higher risk, higher long-term returns

💰Compound Frequency

How compounding frequency affects your returns:

  • Annually — interest calculated once per year
  • Quarterly — interest calculated 4 times per year
  • Monthly — interest calculated 12 times per year

More frequent compounding = higher returns. Over 30 years, monthly compounding can yield significantly more than annual compounding on the same rate.

Example: NZ$10,000 at 5% for 30 years

  • Annual: NZ$43,219
  • Quarterly: NZ$44,677
  • Monthly: NZ$44,823
