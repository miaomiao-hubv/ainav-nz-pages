📘Kāinga Ora First Home Scheme

The First Home Grant gives eligible buyers $5,000–$20,000 towards a first home. The First Home Loan lets you buy with just 5% deposit.

📐Income & Price Caps

Income caps:

  • Single: $95,000
  • Couple / 2+: $150,000

House price caps:

RegionExistingNew
Auckland$875k$975k
Wellington$700k$800k
Christchurch$575k$675k
Rest of NZ$525k$625k

🏠Grant Amounts

Existing: 3yr=$5k, 4yr=$7.5k, 5+yr=$10k per person

New build: 3yr=$10k, 4yr=$15k, 5+yr=$20k per person

Must meet income cap and price cap.

🔗Official Reference

Full eligibility rules at Kāinga Ora website.

kaingaora.govt.nz

⚠️Important Notes

  • Must have contributed to KS 3+ yrs
  • Property must be in NZ and you must live in it
  • First Home Loan rate set by your bank
  • Income is gross (before tax)
