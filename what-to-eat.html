🇳🇿 NZ Popular Cuisines

NZ's food scene blends Pacific, Asian, and European influences. Favourites include fish & chips, meat pies, hangi, and a growing Asian food culture in Auckland and Wellington.

🥦 Healthy Eating NZ

The NZ Ministry of Health recommends 5+ servings of vegetables and 2+ of fruit daily. Look for the Health Star Rating on packaged foods — more stars = healthier choice.
