📘How KiwiSaver Works

KiwiSaver is a voluntary, work-based savings scheme to help you save for your first home or retirement. Employees can choose to contribute 3%, 4%, 6%, 8%, or 10% of their before-tax salary.

📐Key Rules

  • Employer must contribute at least 3%
  • Government contributes 50¢ per $1 you contribute, up to $521.43/year
  • ESCT applies to employer contributions (rates: 10.5%–33%)
  • Contributions are deducted from before-tax salary

💡ESCT Rates (2026)

ESCT (Employer Superannuation Contribution Tax) applies to employer contributions:

≤$16,800: 10.5%

$16,801–$57,600: 17.5%

$57,601–$84,000: 30%

>$84,000: 33%

🔗Official Reference

All KiwiSaver rules and rates are set by the Government and Inland Revenue.
