🇳🇿 NZ English Notes

  • NZ English follows British spelling: colour, organise, centre
  • -ise endings preferred over -ize (organise, not organize)
  • Common Maori words used: kia ora, hui, whanau
  • Date format: day/month/year (25/12/2024)
  • Currency: NZ dollar ($ or NZD)
