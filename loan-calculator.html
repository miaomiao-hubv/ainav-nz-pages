⚖️CCCFA Protections

The Credit Contracts and Consumer Finance Act (CCCFA) protects NZ borrowers:

  • Lenders must assess affordability
  • Full disclosure of all fees & rates
  • Right to repay early (no penalty after 2015)
  • Cooling-off period for door sales
  • Hardship applications if circumstances change

💰Repayment Frequency

More frequent repayments save interest because the principal reduces faster:

  • Fortnightly — most common in NZ (matches pay cycle)
  • Weekly — saves slightly more interest
  • Monthly — standard for personal loans

Tip: Fortnightly on a 25-yr mortgage pays it off ~5 years early!

💸NZ Loan Fees

Common fees charged by NZ lenders:

  • Establishment: NZ$100–500
  • Monthly admin: NZ$0–15
  • Early repayment: NZ$0 (CCCFA)
  • Default/late: NZ$20–50 per event
  • Variation fee: NZ$0–50

ℹ️Getting Help

Before taking a loan, consider:

  • Sorted.org.nz — free budgeting tools
  • Commerce Commission — lender complaints
  • Compare at least 3 lenders before deciding
