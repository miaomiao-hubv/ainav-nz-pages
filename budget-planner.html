📊Budget Rules Explained

50/30/20 Rule: A classic budgeting method — 50% Needs, 30% Wants, 20% Savings.

60/20/20 Rule: NZ adaptation for high housing costs. NZ has some of the highest housing costs relative to income globally, so 60% is allocated to needs (including housing).

  • Needs: rent, food, utilities, transport
  • Wants: entertainment, dining, subscriptions
  • Savings: emergency fund, investments, KiwiSaver

💰NZ Living Costs

Typical NZ household expenses (Stats NZ data):

  • Housing: 25–35% of income
  • Food: 15–18% of income
  • Transport: 10–15% of income
  • Utilities: 5–8% of income
  • Insurance: 3–5% of income
  • Entertainment: 5–10% of income

Source: Stats NZ Household Economic Survey.
