💡 Myth Buster

Many believe secondary income is "punished" with 30% or 33% tax. This is false. NZ uses combined annual progressive tax brackets. The higher rate is just overwithholding by your employer to avoid a big bill at year-end — and IRD automatically refunds the difference!

📘How Secondary Tax Works

Secondary tax applies when you have two or more sources of income. Your main job uses your full tax brackets, while your second job is taxed at a higher rate to prevent underpaying.

At year-end, IRD reconciles your combined income using the standard progressive brackets. Any overpaid tax is automatically refunded.
