🇳🇿 NZ Food Safety

  • "Best Before" ≠ "Use By" — know the difference
  • Food can often be safe after "Best Before" but not "Use By"

🧊 Storage Tips

  • Fridge should be at or below 4°C
  • Freezer should be at or below -18°C
  • Store raw meat on the bottom shelf
  • Keep perishables out of the "danger zone" (4–60°C)
  • First In, First Out (FIFO) rotation method
  • Label containers with the date opened
  • Vacuum sealing can extend shelf life by 2–3x
