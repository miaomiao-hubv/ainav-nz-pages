📋 Repayment Order

❄️Snowball Method

Pay off debts from smallest balance to largest. Quick wins keep you motivated.

  • Best for motivation
  • Faster psychological progress
  • May cost more in interest

🏔️Avalanche Method

Pay off debts from highest interest rate to lowest. Saves the most money.

  • Minimises total interest
  • Fastest overall payoff
  • Requires more discipline
