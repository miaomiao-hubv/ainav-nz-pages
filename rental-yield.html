📘How Rental Yield Works

Gross Yield = (Annual Rent ÷ Property Value) × 100. Net Yield factors in all expenses. A healthy net yield in NZ is typically 3–5%.

📐Key Formulas

Gross Yield:

(Annual Rent / Value) × 100

Net Yield:

((Rent − Expenses) / Value) × 100

Cashflow:

Weekly Rent − Weekly Expenses

💡NZ Expense Benchmarks

  • Maintenance: 0.5–1.5% of value/year
  • Property management: 6–9% of rent
  • Vacancy: 1–3 weeks per year
  • Rates: varies by council
